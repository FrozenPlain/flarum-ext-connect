Flarum extension that lets customers optionally sign in to the [FrozenPlain forum](https://forum.frozenplain.com) with their [frozenplain.com](https://frozenplain.com) account. It's specific to FrozenPlain's setup, so it's unlikely to be useful elsewhere.

Credit to [JWT Cookie Login](https://github.com/clarkwinkelmann/flarum-ext-jwt-cookie-login) for the approach.
